THE STORY

Every Pour Has a Story

Mamta Resin Art began at a small home studio table, with a single mould, a set of pigments, and a lot of patience. What started as a hobby of watching colour swirl and settle grew into a craft — every tray, coaster and keepsake still poured by hand, layer by layer, right there at the same table.

Quality is never rushed here. We work with premium-grade resin chosen for its clarity and depth, cure each piece for as long as it truly needs, and hand-sand and polish every edge before it's considered finished — because a handmade piece should feel like one.

  • Hand-poured in small batches
  • Cured slow, never rushed
  • Finished & polished by hand
